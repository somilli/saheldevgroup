Mission

One group.
Built for the Sahel.

No slogans about changing the world. Just the work, stated plainly.

Sahel Development Group is one company building across architecture, technology, and finance — the three things a place needs before anything else can move. We start where our own people are: Mali, then the wider Sahel.

Based Columbus, Ohio

Run from the United States, where the capital, the customers, and the diaspora are.

Products and services designed for the ground they will actually run on.

How Diaspora-first

Built from abroad, with people on the ground. The diaspora pays, the Sahel benefits.

What we do

Four arms.
One mission.

Tap any arm to open it. Each one is a working direction of the group — stated as it is, not dressed up.

For a family building from abroad, guessing is expensive. SDG Design turns a plot and an idea into a full 3D design — the family sees exactly what their money will build before the first brick is laid.

SDG Pay is the payments arm — sending, receiving, and holding money, built for how people in the Sahel actually do it, including the places the banking system never reached.

SDG Connect puts routers into local businesses and runs the connection as a service — a shop gets Wi-Fi its customers can actually use, without a contract it cannot afford.

Buying land is easy; proving it is yours is the hard part. SDG Certificates gets land buyers their certificates and documents quickly — the paperwork done properly, so ownership is never a question.

Sabaq

An AI Quran memorization coach — the first multi interactive AI Quranic platform.

Memorizing alone is hard to sustain: nobody hears your mistakes, and nobody notices when you stop. Sabaq sits across from you instead — it listens, it corrects, and it keeps the record — so practice keeps moving between lessons, not just during them.

How it works

Recite. Get corrected.
Keep going.

Four pieces, one loop: you recite, the coach responds, and the people who support you can see it working.

01 Live recitation feedback

Sabaq listens while you recite and responds as you go — mistakes surface in the moment, while the verse is still in front of you, not days later in a lesson.

02 Hafs and Warsh

Two readings, both supported. Recite in the riwayah you learned — the coach follows your reading instead of forcing you into someone else's.

03 A dashboard for parents

Parents can follow progress from their own view — what was practiced, how it went, whether the habit is holding — without sitting in on every session.

04 Wherever you are starting from

Made for beginners opening the Quran for the first time, solo learners with no teacher nearby, and adults finding their way back to it after years away.
